# Fynd FinOps Homepage Design Ideas

## Chosen Approach: Deep Space Enterprise Dark

The design faithfully replicates the original Fynd FinOps homepage with its sophisticated deep-space aesthetic.

**Design Movement**: Enterprise Dark / Glassmorphism
**Core Principles**:
- Deep navy/midnight background (#060b18) as the foundation
- Subtle aurora/conic gradient animations for atmospheric depth
- Glassmorphic app cards with frosted borders
- Silver metallic 3D ring decorations as corner accents

**Color Philosophy**: Deep oceanic navy with cool blue-steel accents. The darkness conveys trust, security, and professionalism — appropriate for a financial operations platform.

**Layout Paradigm**: Centered vertically and horizontally, single-column card stack — optimized for a "launcher" / domain selector UX.

**Signature Elements**:
- Rotating aurora background with blur
- Shimmer ring animation on the logo mark
- Silver 3D rounded-rect rings at corners (top-left, bottom-right)

**Typography System**: Inter font, bold display weight for brand title, light muted for subtitles and descriptions.

**Animation**: Entrance animations (fadeUp, containerIn), continuous aurora rotation, shimmer ring, twinkling stars, floating rings.
